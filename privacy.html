Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

TurfGlow Cleaning (“TurfGlow,” “we,” “us”) cleans artificial turf across the Phoenix Valley, Arizona. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and when you contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Quote requests: your name, phone number, email, address and zip code, turf size, the services and plan you’re interested in, how you heard about us, any promo or referral code, and any photos of your turf you choose to upload.
  • Referrals: your name and phone or email, plus the name, phone number and (optionally) address of the people you refer. Please only refer people you think would want to hear from us.
  • Calls, texts and emails: anything you share with us when you contact us.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information such as your browser type, the pages you visit and your approximate location based on your IP address. We also use the Meta Pixel (see “Cookies and the Meta Pixel” below).

How we use your information

  • To prepare your quote and call or text you about it
  • To schedule, perform and bill for your cleanings, including appointment reminders
  • To run our referral program and apply referral credits
  • To send marketing texts, only if you opt in
  • To answer questions and provide customer service
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising, and to prevent spam and fraud

Text messages

When you request a quote, you agree that TurfGlow may call or text you about your quote and service. If you also check the marketing box, we may send you marketing texts about promotions, seasonal offers and special deals (about 2–4 per month). Consent to marketing texts is not required to book. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text-message consent with third parties for their own marketing.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run TurfGlow, and only as needed for them to do that work:

  • Housecall Pro — scheduling, customer records, quotes and payments
  • Form and email delivery services (such as FormSubmit and Resend) — delivering website form submissions to our inbox
  • Vercel — hosting our website
  • Meta Platforms — measuring how our ads perform (through the Meta Pixel)
  • Google — providing the fonts our website uses

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our customers, our team or the public.

Cookies and the Meta Pixel

Our website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta (Facebook and Instagram) that uses cookies to record visits and actions like requesting a quote or tapping to call. It helps us understand which ads work. Meta may also use a scrambled (hashed) version of contact details you type into our forms to match your visit to a Meta account. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and manage ad settings in your Facebook or Instagram account.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as we need it to serve you and to keep proper business records, then delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we have about you by emailing or calling us (contact details below). You can stop marketing texts at any time by replying STOP.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of sending or storing data online is completely secure.

Children

Our services are for adults. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we’ll post the new version here and change the effective date above.
