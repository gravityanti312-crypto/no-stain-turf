Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does turf cleaning cost?

Every yard is different — the price depends on how much turf you have, whether you have pets, and what shape it’s in. Get a free quote in about 30 seconds and we’ll call you with your exact price in minutes.

How do you know how big my yard is?

Just pick the closest size — no measuring needed. We look at your yard on satellite before we call, and we confirm the size with you before any work starts.

Which cleaning do I need?

Refresh Clean is for turf with light use and no pets. Deep Clean is for pet homes and turf that smells. Full Restore brings flat, matted, or never-cleaned turf back to life. Not sure? We’ll recommend one on your call.

How do Turf Club plans work?

Pick monthly, every-other-month, or quarterly cleanings and save $150, $100, or $50 on every cleaning. Plans are auto-scheduled and your price is locked for 12 months. Savings start after your first cleaning, with a $100 minimum per visit. One-time cleanings are always available too.

Is it safe for my pets and kids?

Yes. Every product we use is non-toxic and pet-safe, so your dogs and kids can be back on the turf as soon as it dries.

What areas do you serve?

The Phoenix Valley, including Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Queen Creek, Glendale, Surprise, and Paradise Valley.
